# Vision / multimodal retention under MoE expert pruning and extreme quantization

Scope: how vision survives expert pruning (REAP-class) and low-bit quantization in natively multimodal MoEs; how to calibrate, protect and measure it on one Thor. Internal measurements cite the local wiki (`~/glm-5.3-reap/wiki/`) and memory notes. Licences were re-checked on 2026-10-17 through the HF Hub API (`/api/datasets/<id>` cardData.license); tags are self-declared by the uploader.

## KQ1. How much faster does vision degrade than text under expert pruning, and why?

### Takeaway
The only public VLM-MoE expert-pruning artifact with modality-split numbers (LibertAI's DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash REAP) shows vision collapsing ~5x faster than text past a threshold: at 256/384 experts kept (33% prune) text PPL is +5.5% while caption PPL is +27.4% under the standard modality-agnostic mean-saliency ranking, and text-ONLY saliency ranking at 272/384 (29% prune) is worse on vision than random pruning (+15.9% vs +7.2%) while being indistinguishable on text. The mechanism is modality-dependent expert specialisation (the model literally carries a second router bias `gate.bias_vl`, correlation −0.05 with the text bias), so a text-dominated or text-only importance score deletes experts that are low-rank for text but top-rank for images.

### Cited Findings
- DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash REAP, held-out text PPL (65,504 tokens): 384 experts 3.6552; 272 kept 3.8060 (+4.13%); 256 (mean saliency) 3.8557 (+5.49%); 240 3.9156 (+7.12%); 192 4.1910 (+14.66%) `[MEAS]` — [LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-REAP-256E](https://huggingface.co/LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-REAP-256E)
- Same model, caption PPL (2,048 tokens, image-conditioned): unpruned 24.307; 272 kept text+image saliency 24.769 (+1.9%); 272 random 26.066 (+7.2%); 272 text-only saliency 28.173 (+15.9%); 264 mean saliency 30.070 (~+23.7%); 256 mean saliency 30.959 (+27.4%); 256 "balanced" (per-modality-normalised, 50/50) 26.479 (+8.9%) at a text cost (text PPL 4.1723 = +14.1% vs 3.8557) `[MEAS]` — [LibertAIDAI card](https://huggingface.co/LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-REAP-256E)
- Card wording: "Below 272 the vision path falls off a cliff that text perplexity gives no warning of"; "At 272 kept, the resulting checkpoint [text-only ranking] is worse than randomly pruning the same number of experts on vision"; text PPL for text-only vs combined ranking at 304 kept was 3.7300 vs 3.7359 — "Only an image-conditioned evaluation catches it." — [LibertAIDAI card](https://huggingface.co/LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-REAP-256E)
- Why: "this model routes vision separately" — two router biases (`gate.bias`, `gate.bias_vl`), correlation −0.05 (min −0.45 across layers); "Rank by text saliency alone and you discard 267 experts that a combined ranking keeps", e.g. layer-0 expert 235 is "376th of 384 on text, 21st on images". Calibration: 262,144 text tokens + 256 images (also quoted as 64.4M text vs 13.0M image tokens in the ranking section — the card does not reconcile the two). Experts stay MXFP4 (E2M1+E8M0 block 32), dense FP8, Engram untouched. Recipe repo: https://github.com/Libertai/deepseek-v41-flash-reap — [LibertAIDAI card](https://huggingface.co/LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-REAP-256E)
- vLLM's fused-MoE kernel rejects arbitrary expert counts ("Unsupported expert number: 272"); 256 is the largest legal count below 384, which is why the shipped artifact sits exactly at the vision cliff — [LibertAIDAI card](https://huggingface.co/LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-REAP-256E)
- Modality-dependent sensitivity to removing experts (dynamic skipping, not permanent pruning, but same direction): MoDES finds "experts have a larger effect on updating text tokens"; FFNs change vision tokens less (higher pre/post-FFN cosine); reducing top-k for vision tokens degrades task scores more slowly than for text tokens (Appendix G, figure-only). Kimi-VL-A3B at 83% experts skipped with modality-specific thresholds: TextVQA 82.38 (vs 88.70 default), ChartQA 84.20 (89.48), MMBench 81.44 (83.16), MME 2162 (2207), 96.25% relative; single-threshold ablation collapses MMBench to 65.48 vs 79.42 with per-modality thresholds. Qwen3-VL-MoE-30B-A3B at 88% skipped: TextVQA 80.97 (83.41), ChartQA 78.84 (85.08) `[MEAS]` — [MoDES arXiv 2511.15690](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.15690v2)
- FastMMoE routing analysis (InternVL3.5-30B-A3B, 128 experts top-8; DeepSeek-VL2): text tokens have higher top-1 routing probability than vision tokens in nearly all layers (text 0.27–0.30 vs vision 0.16–0.19 on InternVL3.5; DeepSeek-VL2 text 0.35–0.41 in some layers vs vision 0.17–0.19); vision routing is flatter (top-K/2 mass 0.54–0.58 vs >0.60 text); consecutive visual tokens' routing cosine >95% in shallow layers; halving experts activated for visual tokens from layer 2 keeps ~99.2% of performance on InternVL3.5, but on DeepSeek-VL2 also reducing SHARED experts drops OCRBench 81.10 → 70.80 `[MEAS]` — [FastMMoE arXiv 2511.17885](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.17885v1)
- KAB-MoE (NeurIPS 2026): "substantial visual expert redundancy"; retains 97.09% average on Kimi-VL-A3B at 83% visual-expert skipping — [NeurIPS poster 151613](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/151613)
- LIMoE (2022, contrastive image-text MoE): "Image tokens are spread across nearly all experts, so image experts are much harder to prune"; some experts mostly image, some mostly text, some both — [LIMoE arXiv 2206.02770](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.02770); [Google blog](https://research.google/blog/limoe-learning-multiple-modalities-with-one-sparse-mixture-of-experts-model/)
- Internal (GLM-5.3-Flash 288 experts, shipped REAP-50): every expert fired in every layer under the mixed corpus (min 854 / median ~7,200 / max 216,385 tokens per expert at layer 8, ~250x spread) — [wiki/00-log.md](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/wiki/00-log.md); retained saliency mass vision 0.682 (mean criterion) / 0.755 (mass criterion), mid-pack among seven buckets — [wiki/97-evaluation.md](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/wiki/97-evaluation.md); [memory: reap-criterion-and-corpus-audit](/home/patrickd/.claude/projects/-home-patrickd/memory/reap-criterion-and-corpus-audit.md)

### Inferences
- `[EXT]` The two literatures are not contradictory: vision tokens are individually REDUNDANT per token (flat routing, lower FFN effect → aggressive per-token skipping is cheap) but collectively DIVERSE across experts (LIMoE "spread across nearly all experts"; V4.1 `bias_vl`). Permanent pruning removes the long tail that image tokens vote for weakly-but-broadly; per-token skipping does not. So skipping papers' "95–97% retained at 83% skip" must NOT be read as "vision tolerates 50% expert deletion".
- `[EXT]` The V4.1 cliff between 272 and 256 (29% → 33%) is a threshold, not a slope: once the modality-specific periphery is exhausted, each further expert removed is a vision expert. At the project's 50% target on a mean-saliency ranking the vision loss is unbounded by any published number; the only measured 50% point (192E) has no vision metric.
- `[EXT]` Ratio of damage at 256E: 27.4 / 5.49 = ~5x faster in PPL terms under mean saliency; the per-modality-normalised ranking reduces that to 8.9 / 14.1 (vision now better than text) — i.e. the trade is tunable, and a 50/50 weight over-corrects for a text-first deployment.
- `[EXT]` GLM-5.3-Flash's "every expert fired" under a 15%-image corpus is consistent with LIMoE-style diffusion; it rules out zero-saliency deletion but says nothing about whether the SURVIVING vision mass is enough — exactly the state wiki/97 records as "unmeasured".

### Gaps
- No paper tabulates text vs vision at 25/50/60% permanent expert pruning for Kimi-VL, Qwen3-VL-MoE, Llama-4, GLM-4.5V/5V, MiMo-VL or InternVL-MoE; searches for REAP cards of those models returned none (Cerebras collection has GLM-4.5-Air/4.6/4.7-Flash/Qwen3-Coder/Kimi-Linear only; kwanhee Kimi-K2.5-REAP50 reports text-only evals) — [Cerebras HF collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/cerebras); [Kimi-K2.5-REAP50 card](https://huggingface.co/kwanhee/Kimi-K2.5-REAP50-INT4-W4A16)
- No per-layer image-vs-text routing concentration or Jaccard overlap statistic exists for DeepSeek-V4.x, MiMo-V2.6 or GLM-5.3-Flash; LibertAI gives only the bias correlation and one anecdotal expert. The project's own banked saliency (`sum_by_bucket`) can produce this offline for GLM-5.3-Flash at zero GPU.
- noooop's DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 REAP50 (256→128) card has no vision mention; base 0731 is text-only (vision arrived with V4-Flash-Vision-Exp 2026-08-21 and was folded into V4.1-Flash 2026-09-10), so "0909 REAP" vision claims trace to the LibertAI V4.1 card, not noooop — [noooop card](https://huggingface.co/noooop/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-REAP-noMTP); [V4.1 Flash launch](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/11/deepseek-v4-1-flash-launches-with-lower-prices-and-native-vision/)

## KQ2. Modality-aware saliency and protection methods (MAEP, modality-specific experts, per-modality routing-mass protection)

### Takeaway
Three concrete recipes exist: (a) LibertAI's per-modality saliency normalisation + weighted combination (measured: fixes the cliff, 30.96 → 26.48 caption PPL at 256E); (b) MAEP (NeurIPS 2026) — a Delta-H output-change score decomposed by modality, vision-side importance drives pruning while text-side importance acts as a preservation guard, global cross-layer ranking (numbers not retrievable: OpenReview blocked, no arXiv); (c) our own `--protect-frac` per-bucket top-slice protection, which is the same idea applied as a hard floor. Nothing published is a "vision-protected REAP" release other than LibertAI's 256E.

### Cited Findings
- MAEP abstract: "Existing pruning methods aggregate importance uniformly across tokens, which overlooks that one expert can serve different roles for vision and language processing"; Delta-H Output = "redistribution-aware metric measuring the MoE output change when an expert is removed"; low-attention vision tokens used as a dataset-stable pruning signal; "vision-side importance drives pruning, text-side importance preserves experts, and experts are ranked globally across layers"; models InternVL3.5-30B-A3B and Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B (+1 unnamed); removes ~27 GB of ~54 GB BF16 expert weight (~47% of memory, fits one 40 GB GPU); "outperforms modality-agnostic baselines on the majority of multimodal benchmarks while remaining competitive on text-only tasks" — [NeurIPS 2026 poster 148334](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/148334) (OpenReview id ycGTW508pr; PDF behind a bot-check, no arXiv found)
- LibertAI "balanced" ranking = accumulate image and text saliency separately, normalise each to equal per-layer mean, average 50/50; at 256E caption PPL 26.479 vs 30.959 for plain mean saliency; the card also recommends the text+image combined ranking at 272 (+1.9% vision, +4.1% text) as the best point if the serving stack allowed it — [LibertAIDAI card](https://huggingface.co/LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-REAP-256E)
- MoDES dual-modality thresholds (separate skip thresholds for vision and text tokens) vs one global threshold at 83% skip on Kimi-VL: MMBench 79.42 vs 65.48, LVB 61.16 vs 54.67 — modality-blind thresholds are the failure — [MoDES arXiv 2511.15690](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.15690v2)
- SMoES (CVPR 2026, Li Auto): routing guided by layer-dependent soft modality scores + expert binning; +0.9% multimodal, +4.2% language-only, −56.1% EP communication across 4 MoE-VLMs / 16 benchmarks — training-time, so a design reference not a post-hoc tool — [SMoES arXiv 2604.23996](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.23996)
- Internal: MiMo-V2.6-Flash REAP pass per-domain expert coverage after 5 chunks — video 35.4% mean / 6.2% min (16 of 256 experts in its worst layer) on the same token count as audio (59.2% / 21.5%) → diversity not budget; "`--protect-frac` exists for this: protect the top slice of every domain separately, or a global ranking silently drops the experts only one modality uses" — [memory: media-corpus-sources-silently-dead](/home/patrickd/.claude/projects/-home-patrickd/memory/media-corpus-sources-silently-dead.md)
- Internal: bucket-separable saliency `S_j = Σ_b w_b · (sum_by_bucket[b,j] / cnt_by_bucket[b,j])` is already banked per bucket for GLM-5.3-Flash, so a LibertAI-style per-modality re-weighting costs zero GPU — [wiki/80-calibration.md](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/wiki/80-calibration.md)

### Inferences
- `[EXT]` LibertAI's per-modality normalisation is exactly our banked `sum_by_bucket/cnt_by_bucket` with `w_vision` raised; the measured 50/50 over-corrects (+14% text). A text-first deployment should sweep `w_vision` ∈ {0.15, 0.25, 0.35} and pick on a paired image-conditioned eval, not by assertion.
- `[EXT]` MAEP's "vision drives pruning, text preserves" is the mirror of what we want (we deploy text-first with vision as a protected domain); its transferable piece is the per-modality decomposition and global cross-layer ranking — the latter is NOT shippable for glm5_next / vLLM (single scalar `num_local_experts`) per memory, so only the per-layer modality split carries over.
- `[EXT]` A hard floor (`protect-frac` top-K vision experts per layer) is the only mechanism that bounds vision loss independent of the text/vision weight; the weighting alone can still delete a dual-role expert whose combined score lands below the cut.

### Gaps
- MAEP's result tables, prune ratios, calibration set size and any rank-correlation evidence for "dual-role" mis-ranking are unavailable (OpenReview bot-check; no arXiv preprint found by title or author).
- No published "vision-protected REAP" at 50% exists; LibertAI stops at 33%.

## KQ3. What share of calibration should be image-text, measured in routed tokens; do image tokens crowd out text?

### Takeaway
The one measured data point: 13.0M image tokens vs 64.4M text (16.8% image share by routed token) with a plain mean-saliency ranking was NOT enough to prevent the V4.1 vision cliff at 33% prune, but a per-modality normalisation at the same share fixed it — i.e. share matters less than how the modality is weighted, because image tokens have lower per-token gate mass and flatter routing and therefore under-vote per token. Crowd-out is real in the other direction: LibertAI's 50/50 weighting cost +8.6 PPL points on text. Our GLM pipeline specified multimodal at 15% of SAMPLES, which realised as 6.2% of TOKENS at the pass-2 first layer dump; the "434 image-text / 15.85% of routed slots" figure in the assignment is not recorded in the wiki pages I could find.

### Cited Findings
- V4.1 calibration: 262,144 text tokens + 256 images (config) / 64.4M text vs 13.0M image tokens (ranking section) → plain mean saliency ranking "is text-heavy" and produced the 256E cliff; same data, per-modality normalised, resolved it — [LibertAIDAI card](https://huggingface.co/LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-REAP-256E)
- Vision tokens route flatter and with lower top-1 gate mass than text (InternVL3.5 top-1 0.16–0.19 vs 0.27–0.30) — [FastMMoE](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.17885v1); MBQ: gradient of the loss w.r.t. language tokens is >10x that of vision tokens (LLaVA-OneVision-7B, 13th block) — [MBQ arXiv 2412.19509](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19509v2)
- Internal: mixture specified in samples acts in tokens — multimodal intended 15% (samples) realised 6.2% (tokens) at pass-2 layer-1 dump; code 21% → 5.7%, finance 8% → 0.5% — [wiki/80-calibration.md](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/wiki/80-calibration.md)
- Internal: GLM-5.3-Flash image record = 208–256 image placeholder tokens per 448x448 image (256 for the full canvas); held-out image-text records average ~3,450 placeholder positions vs ~19 real text tokens — [wiki/00-log.md](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/wiki/00-log.md); [wiki/97-evaluation.md](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/wiki/97-evaluation.md)
- Internal: pass-1 vision was calibrated on only 39 image samples; a re-run draws 42; multimodal corpus kept at 128 image-text records (2 shards) after reclaim — [specialist_strategy.md sibling note](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/research_notes/Compressing 1T MoE for Thor/specialist_strategy.md); [wiki/00-log.md](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/wiki/00-log.md)
- Internal: 9 of 15 configured image sources yielded ZERO images (path-only: all Nemotron-VLM-v2 configs, aguvis-stage2, BigDocs GUI-VQA, pixmo-cap, blip3-ocr); per-source weights were ignored (first live source filled the bucket) — [memory: media-corpus-sources-silently-dead](/home/patrickd/.claude/projects/-home-patrickd/memory/media-corpus-sources-silently-dead.md)
- Internal: corpus visit order was blocked by domain, placing media at 74–85% of the pass — [memory: calibration-corpus-visit-order](/home/patrickd/.claude/projects/-home-patrickd/memory/calibration-corpus-visit-order.md)
- Sibling note's dose-response recommendation: vision 14% (parity) budgeted in routed LLM tokens including image tokens, ≥500k routed tokens per pool; "Whether vision's routed-token count should include image tokens at full weight is unmeasured" — [calibration_mixture_dose_response.md](/home/patrickd/glm-5.3-reap/research_notes/Compressing 1T MoE for Thor/calibration_mixture_dose_response.md)
- MBQ (quantization, not pruning): switching calibration from Pile (text) to COCO (image-text) moved AWQ-W3 MMMU 36.6 → 38.7 and SEED 51.5 → 61.8 but SmoothQuant-W4A8 SEED 41.6 → 10.2 — image-text calibration helps or wrecks depending on whether the method weights modalities — [MBQ arXiv 2412.19509](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19509v2)

### Inferences
- `[EXT]` Count image placeholder tokens as routed tokens (they ARE routed through every MoE layer), but weight the modality's saliency, not its token count: the V4.1 evidence says ~17% image routed-token share with mean-criterion weighting still lost vision, and what fixed it was per-modality normalisation. Target: 15–20% image routed tokens AND a per-bucket normalised weight `w_vision` ≥ its token share, swept on an image-conditioned eval.
- `[EXT]` Under REAP's mean (1/N_j) criterion image tokens cannot "crowd out" text saliency through count (the mean is frequency-invariant); under our mass criterion they can, and raising vision share raises the diffuse set broadly (LIMoE) — so the mass criterion + high image share is the combination that would hurt text specialists. The banked per-bucket accumulators let both be tried offline.
- `[EXT]` 39–128 images is an order of magnitude below LibertAI's 256 images, which the card itself calls too small; given 256 tokens/image on GLM-5.3 vs 1,024/image on V4.1, ~1,000+ images (~250k–1M image tokens) is the floor for routed-token parity at 15%.
- `[EXT]` The two V4.1 token counts (262k vs 64.4M text) differ by 245x; one is probably the per-pass config and the other the accumulated total across chunks — treat the 16.8% share as approximate.

### Gaps
- The assignment's "2,252 text + 434 image-text samples, vision ~15.85% of routed slots" is not in `wiki/50-multimodal.md`, `80-calibration.md`, `97-evaluation.md` or the memory notes (grep for 434 / 2,252 / 15.85 found nothing); the wiki's recorded numbers are 431 text + 81 image-text drawn for a 512-sample s03 run, 15% sample share → 6.2% token share, and 39 images in pass 1. If 15.85% is the pass-3 figure it should be written into the wiki with its token basis.
- No paper reports a dose-response curve of image-text calibration share vs vision retention under expert pruning.

## KQ4. Vision encoder / projector handling: does quantizing the tower or the LM's attention to NVFP4 / 3-bit break vision first?

### Takeaway
Quantizing the vision ENCODER is measured to cost ~0–1 point (W4A8 ViT on LLaVA-OV-7B: MMMU 43.1 vs 42.6 with FP16 ViT; W4A4 ViT on Qwen2.5-VL-7B: MMMU 46.6 vs 46.9) — and at 0.5–0.9 GiB per tower it buys nothing, so keep it BF16 for margin. The real precision risk is in the LANGUAGE model at ≤4-bit with text-calibrated quantizers: W4A8 SmoothQuant drops LLaVA-OV-7B MMMU 46.0 → 30.9 and TextVQA 76.1 → 56.9 while MMLU drops only 65.9 → 59.8; W3A16 AWQ drops MMMU 46.0 → 36.6 vs MMLU 65.9 → 61.9. Vision tasks lose 2–3x more than text at the same LM bit-width when calibration ignores modality.

- MBQ (CVPR 2025), LLaVA-OneVision-7B FP16: MMMU 46.0, TextVQA 76.1, MMLU 65.9. W3A16: AWQ MMMU 36.6 / TextVQA 73.0 / MMLU 61.9; GPTQ 41.9 / 71.3; RTN 34.7 / 60.9; MBQ 42.0 / 73.3 / 62.9. ## KQ5. Evaluation: which multimodal benchmarks detect pruning damage cheaply on one Thor; minimum items for a 2-point MDE; which runtimes can run the vision tower

### Takeaway
A 2-point drop is NOT detectable by two independent runs on any single benchmark of ≤1,000 items (needs ~7,700 items/arm at p=0.5); it IS detectable with a PAIRED, greedy, same-item comparison when the two models disagree on ≤5–10% of items (~770–1,550 items). So run teacher and student on the identical item set, score per item, and use McNemar — ChartQA-test (2,500), DocVQA-val (5,349), TextVQA-val (5,000) are the cheap high-n OCR/chart detectors; MMMU-val (900) and MathVista-testmini (1,000) only catch ≥3–4-point drops. The V4.1 cliff (+27% caption PPL) is far above these floors; a 2,048-token caption-PPL check caught it. Runtime status: llama.cpp mtmd has DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp (PR #28133, merged 2026-09-02, Metal/CPU-tested, no CUDA/Jetson test, no video, no mixed image+text ubatch), nothing found for V4.1-Flash or MiMo-V2.6; vLLM carries the V4.1 vision path (`--language-model-only` exists to disable it) and vLLM already serves vision on Thor for Gemma-4.

## Direct answers to the four key questions

### Takeaway
(1) Text-vs-vision deltas exist for exactly one VLM-MoE at the same prune ratio (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, tabulated above; nothing at 50%/60%). (2) Image routing: flatter and lower top-1 than text (FastMMoE numbers), modality-dependent specialisation (V4.1 `bias_vl` corr −0.05; 267 experts change under text-only ranking), shallow layers most modality-stable; no per-layer Jaccard anywhere. (3) MAEP: method known, numbers not retrievable; LibertAI's balanced ranking is the only measured vision-protected REAP. (4) Our GLM pipeline: vision retention 0.682/0.755 of saliency mass, every expert fired, but the shipped REAP-50 has NO vision measurement (532 scorable tokens) and the assignment's 434-sample / 15.85% figure is not in the wiki. (5) V4.1-Flash vision: 32-layer ViT, 0.90 GiB, ≤1,024 tokens/image; MiMo-V2.6: 28-layer 1280-wide ViT, 1.38 GiB, 2x2 merge; "degrades much faster" quantified to +27.4% caption PPL vs +5.5% text PPL at 256/384.
